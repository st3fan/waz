# Objective

## Purpose

WAZ (Wat Aten Zij) helps families decide what to eat together through a shared meal plan. Its name is a play on an old Dutch radio show about what people had for dinner.

## Problem and audience

Families plan meals around several people's tastes, schedules, and past experiences. WAZ serves a family whose members can each sign in, contribute to the plan, and change it.

## Desired outcomes

- WAZ fills the meal plan automatically, reducing the work of deciding what to eat.
- Every family member has meaningful input and can edit the shared plan.
- Plans reflect preferences, family calendar context, past choices, and likes or dislikes, and improve as members give input.

## Direction

Use an AI-driven backend to fill a shared meal plan from family context. Keep the plan editable and use family feedback to inform future choices.

## Scope and non-goals

WAZ focuses on meal planning. Adjacent tasks, including shopping lists, are outside its scope.

## Guiding principles

- Make planning a family collaboration in which every member's input matters.
- Keep family members in control of changes to the automatically filled plan.
- Ground future plans in the family's context and feedback.
